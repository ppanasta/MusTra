## MusTra for Android (1.2.1): new logo

MusTra now has its own icon: a **canary pick**, a yellow plectrum-shaped canary on a purple tile, with the **M** as its wing and the **T** as its perch. You'll see it in your app list, on the home screen and on the welcome page.

This is a temporary logo; the final one is coming in a later version. Nothing else changed since 1.2.0.

### Install or update

1. On your phone, download **`MusTra-android.apk`** below.
2. Open it. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to **install unknown apps** (Settings → Apps → Special access).
3. Tap **Install** (or **Update** if you already have 1.2.0).

If you have MusTra for Android 1.2.0 from the permanent-key release, this installs as an update and keeps your songs and corrections. If you installed a copy before 13 October 2026 (temporary key), uninstall it once first; use *Export* to keep your songs.

The app isn't on Google Play, so Android may show a warning for apps from outside the store. Requires Android 7.0 or newer.
