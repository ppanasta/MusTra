## What's new in 1.1.0: song suggestions

Every dromos and makam page now suggests a song in that dromos or makam, taken from the literature, with a link to the source it came from.

- **57 songs to start with.** Rebetiko songs with the dromos and key given in repertoire lists (e.g. *Το κουτσαβάκι*, Delias 1936, Hitzaz on D; *Ο χαρμάνης*, Vamvakaris 1933, Hitzazkiar), and Turkish works from collections organised by makam (e.g. *Kâtibim*, Nihavend).
- **Another song** shows a different suggestion; the list under it shows every song for that dromos/makam. A dromos page also lists songs from its parent makam, and a makam page lists songs from its dromoi.
- **Wrong dromos?** If a song is filed under the wrong dromos/makam, say which one it is really in, and optionally suggest a more suitable song. The song then also appears under the scale you named, marked as a user correction, and is suggested less often where it is disputed.
- **Suggest a song** for any dromos or makam, with an optional source.
- **Export my contributions** saves your suggestions and reports to a JSON file you can share.

Your songs and corrections are kept in a small database on your computer (on Windows: `%APPDATA%\MaqamBuilder\songs.sqlite3`). They are not shared with other users yet, so use the export to send them in.

Most Greek attributions come from forum repertoire lists compiled by musicians, so some may be debatable. That is what *Wrong dromos?* is for. The Segah, Hicazkâr and Nev'eser makams have no songs yet: be the first to add one.

No installation and no Python needed.
