## What's new in 1.2.0: Arabic maqamat

Maqam Builder now covers three traditions: Greek dromoi, Turkish makams and **Arabic maqamat**, and every page names the equivalent scale in the other two.

- **New Arabic tab with 13 maqamat:** Rast, Bayati, Husseini, Kurd, Hijaz, Hijaz Kar, Saba, Huzam, Sikah, Bayati Shuri (Qarjighar), Nawa Athar, Ajam and Nahawand. They are written in quarter tones (E½♭ = E half-flat), with Arabic note names (Rast, Dukah, Sikah, Jaharkah, Nawa…), step sizes, cents and Hz, ascending and descending.
- **Equivalents everywhere:** e.g. Greek *Ousak* = Turkish *Uşşak* = Arabic *Bayati*, and *Hitzaz* = *Hicaz* = *Hijaz*. The Arabic page compares its maqam note by note with the Turkish makam and the Greek dromos.
- **Arabic column on dromos pages:** next to the Turkish makam, so you can see both references for each fret. The playing tips also say where Arabic notation puts a note. For example, Ousak's E♭: Turkish theory puts it 81¢ above the fret, and Arabic Bayati 50¢, a value many players actually use.
- **Identify understands quarter tones:** type `D Ed F G` (Ed = E half-flat) to get Bayati, Husseini and Bayati Shuri. Plain notes also list the Arabic equivalents of the matching dromoi and makams.
- **Song suggestions** on Arabic pages come from the same family (the equivalent Turkish makam and Greek dromos).

Arabic notation rounds every note to a quarter tone. Real intonation of the half-flat notes varies by region and performer, which is why the Turkish and Arabic values differ.
