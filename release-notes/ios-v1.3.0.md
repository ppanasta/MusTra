## MusTra for iPhone and iPad (1.3.0): correct the theory

Found a mistake in a dromos, makam or Arabic maqam? Every page now has a **Correct the theory** button.

- **What you can correct:** the tonic, the steps or frets, the dominant degree, and the parent or counterpart makam, plus a comment saying what is wrong and how you know.
- **Your copy uses it everywhere:** tables, playing tips, equivalents and *Identify* all follow your correction, and pages that depend on it say "✎ Your corrections are in use".
- **Every version is kept:** each save, roll-back or reset adds a version, and nothing is deleted. Open the dialog to see them all and go back to any version, or to the original theory.
- **Export** now includes your corrections.

Your corrections are **suggestions**: they change only your copy of MusTra. Coming in a future release: send them to the MusTra team by email. An admin will review each suggestion and, if it is right, include it for everyone in a release.

### Install or update

Open **https://ppanasta.github.io/MusTra/** in **Safari**, tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.

Already installed? The app updates itself the next time you open it online; your songs and corrections are kept. They live in Safari's storage for the app, so use *Export* before removing the app or clearing website data.

### Files

`MusTra-web.zip` is the complete web app, for hosting it yourself on any static web server. You don't need it to use MusTra.
