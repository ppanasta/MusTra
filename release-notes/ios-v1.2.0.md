## MusTra for iPhone and iPad (1.2.0)

MusTra now runs on iPhone and iPad as a web app you add to your home screen. It's the same MusTra as the desktop 1.2.0: Greek dromoi, Turkish makams and Arabic maqamat, with equivalents, playing tips and song suggestions.

### Install

1. Open **https://ppanasta.github.io/MusTra/** in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with the arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. Open MusTra from the home screen. The first start downloads about 15 MB, the Python engine that does the calculations. After that it works offline.

### What's inside

- **Dromos, Makam, Arabic and Identify** tabs. Type the first notes to get the name, e.g. `D Eb F# G` or `D Ed F G` (Ed = E half-flat).
- **Tables** you can scroll sideways: each dromos with its Turkish makam and Arabic maqam, cent differences and playing tips (↑ bend up, ↓ hold low).
- **Equivalents** you can tap to jump between traditions, e.g. Ousak → Uşşak → Bayati.
- **Song suggestions** with sources. *Wrong?* lets you give the right dromos/makam and suggest a better song; *Export* saves your contributions.

Your songs and corrections are stored on this device, in Safari's storage for the app. Removing the app from the home screen or clearing website data deletes them, so export first.

### Files

`MusTra-web.zip` is the complete web app, for hosting it yourself on any static web server. You don't need it to use MusTra.
