## MusTra for iPhone and iPad (1.2.1): new logo

MusTra now has its own icon: a **canary pick**, a yellow plectrum-shaped canary on a purple tile, with the **M** as its wing and the **T** as its perch. It's the home-screen icon and appears on the welcome page.

This is a temporary logo; the final one is coming in a later version. Nothing else changed since 1.2.0.

### Install or update

1. Open **https://ppanasta.github.io/MusTra/** in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with the arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.

Already installed? The app updates itself the next time you open it online. iOS keeps the home-screen icon from when you added it, so to see the new icon: *Export* your songs if you've added any, remove MusTra from the home screen, and add it again from Safari. Removing the icon can also clear the app's storage, which is why you should export first.

### Files

`MusTra-web.zip` is the complete web app, for hosting it yourself on any static web server. You don't need it to use MusTra.
