## What's new in 1.3.0: correct the theory

Found a mistake in a dromos, makam or Arabic maqam? Every page now has a **Correct the theory** button.

- **What you can correct:**
  - Greek dromos: the tonic, the frets going up and coming down, and the parent makam.
  - Turkish makam: the tonic, the steps in komas (they must add up to 53) and the güçlü.
  - Arabic maqam: the tonic, the steps in quarter tones (they must add up to 24), the ghammaz and the Turkish counterpart.
- **Say why:** add a comment with what is wrong and how you know (your source).
- **Your copy uses it everywhere:** the tables, playing tips, equivalents and *Identify* all follow your correction. Pages that depend on it say "✎ Your corrections are in use". For example, correcting makam Uşşak also changes the tips for dromos Ousak.
- **Every version is kept:** each save, roll-back or reset adds a version, and nothing is deleted. The dialog lists them all; choose *Use the selected version* to go back to any of them, including the original theory.
- **Export** now includes your corrections, with the original and suggested values and your comments.

Your corrections are **suggestions**: they change only your copy of MusTra. Coming in a future release: send them to the MusTra team by email. An admin will review each suggestion and, if it is right, include it for everyone in a release.

From the command line: `correct`, `history` and `rollback`, e.g. `MusTra-windows.exe correct makam ussak --steps "7 6 9 9 4 9 9" --comment "…"`.
