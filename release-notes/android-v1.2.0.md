## MusTra for Android (1.2.0)

MusTra now runs on Android phones and tablets as an app. It's the same MusTra as the desktop 1.2.0: Greek dromoi, Turkish makams and Arabic maqamat, with equivalents, playing tips and song suggestions. It works fully offline.

### Install

1. On your phone, download **`MusTra-android.apk`** below.
2. Open it. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to **install unknown apps** (Settings → Apps → Special access).
3. Tap **Install**. MusTra appears in your app list.

The app isn't on Google Play, so Android may show a warning for apps from outside the store. Requires Android 7.0 or newer.

### What's inside

- **Dromos, Makam, Arabic and Identify** tabs. Type the first notes to get the name, e.g. `D Eb F# G` or `D Ed F G` (Ed = E half-flat).
- **Tables** you can scroll sideways: each dromos with its Turkish makam and Arabic maqam, cent differences and playing tips (↑ bend up, ↓ hold low).
- **Equivalents** you can tap to jump between traditions, e.g. Ousak → Uşşak → Bayati.
- **Song suggestions** with sources. *Wrong?* lets you give the right dromos/makam and suggest a better song; *Export* shares your contributions through any app.
- The **Back** button returns to the welcome page; **End** closes the app.

Your songs and corrections are stored in the app.
