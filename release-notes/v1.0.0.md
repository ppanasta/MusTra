## MusTra 1.0.0: first release

A small desktop program for musicians who play Greek rebetiko and Ottoman/Turkish makam music. It shows the notes of each Greek **dromos** (δρόμος) and the Turkish **makam** it comes from, and tells you how to bend the fretted notes so the dromos moves like the original makam.

### What it does

- **Dromoi (12 equal semitones, as on the bouzouki):** Matzore, Minore, Rast, Ousak, Kiourdi, Hitzaz, Hitzazkiar, Sabah, Houzam, Segah, Karsigar, Neveser and Piraeus. Notes are shown with Western and Greek names (Ρε, Μι♭ …), cents and Hz, ascending and descending.
- **Makams (53-koma Turkish theory):** Rast, Uşşak, Hüseyni, Kürdi, Hicaz, Hicazkâr, Saba, Hüzzam, Segah, Karcığar, Nev'eser, Çargah and Nihavend. Each note is shown with its perde name (Rast, Dügah, Segah …), step size, komas, cents and Hz.
- **Playing tips:** each dromos is compared note by note with its makam, so you see where to play higher (↑ bend up) or lower (↓ hold it low) and by how many cents. For example, Ousak's E♭ is much higher than the fret, and in Hitzaz the E♭ is slightly high and the F♯ slightly low. The tips also show which notes lean toward the tonic or the dominant.
- **Identify:** type the first notes (e.g. `D Eb F# G`) and get the matching dromoi and makams.
- **Any tonic:** transpose any scale (e.g. Hitzaz on A), and set your own A4 tuning.

### How to use

The program opens on a welcome page with instructions, **Start** and **End** buttons, and clickable references. Press **Start**, choose *Dromos*, *Makam* or *Identify*, pick a name, and the notes and tips appear.

The same file also works on the command line when given arguments, e.g. `MaqamBuilder-windows.exe dromos ousak`.

### Good to know

- Makam pitches follow the theoretical Arel-Ezgi-Uzdilek values. Performers often play some notes, such as Uşşak's segah, lower than the theory, so use your ears.
- Charts of the dromoi differ between teachers; this release follows common rebetiko practice.
